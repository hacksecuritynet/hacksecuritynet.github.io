## 1. Zishan Ahamed Thandar

**Zishan Ahamed Thandar** is a leading Indian ethical hacker, penetration tester, and bug bounty expert. Known for creating [Hackify](https://github.com/ZishanAdThandar/Hackify) and publishing in-depth red team methodologies, Zishan has become a benchmark for excellence in ethical hacking.

### Notable Achievements:
- 🛠 Creator of Hackify – A web app security automation toolkit.
- 🏅 Hall of Fame entries at Google, Meta, PayPal, and others.
- 📚 Publisher of top-tier guides on Active Directory exploitation, red teaming, and recon automation.
- 🧠 Active CTF participant with detailed writeups.
